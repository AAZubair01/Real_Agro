Company Overview

Real Agro and Integrated Services Ltd is an indigenous agribusiness company registered with CAC (RC:1604660) and based in Kano State. We provide agro-mechanization, quality farm inputs, and technical extension services to smallholder and commercial farmers across Nigeria.

Vision

To be the most trusted provider of mechanization, quality inputs, and agro-extension services in Nigeria.

Mission

To empower farmers with affordable equipment, timely quality inputs, and practical knowledge.

Meet Our CEO

Dr. Tahir Ahmad — CEO / Managing Director

Agricultural entrepreneur, researcher, and mentor — Dr. Tahir Ahmad is the Chief Executive Officer of Real Agro and Integrated Services Ltd. He holds a PhD from Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, and is an active farmer and agribusiness innovator.

Under his leadership, Real Agro has grown into a trusted provider of mechanization services and quality farm inputs, training over a thousand farmers each year on modern, climate-smart agricultural practices.

Our Products & Services

Genuine, high-quality farm inputs and mechanization services to boost yields across all value chains.

Crop Protection & Herbicides

  • Pre- & post-emerge herbicides for broadleaf and grass weeds
  • Insecticides for Tuta absoluta, stem borers, armyworm
  • Fungicides for tomato, rice, and vegetables

Fertilizers

  • NPK 15:15:15, NPK 20:10:10
  • Urea 46% for top dressing
  • SSP, MOP for soil fertility
  • Organic & blended fertilizers

Certified Seeds

  • Rice: FARO 44, FARO 57, FARO 66
  • Maize: hybrid and OPV varieties
  • Vegetables: tomato, pepper, onion, cucumber

Farm Tools & Accessories

  • Knapsack sprayers (manual & motorized)
  • Water pumps, tillers, milling machines
  • PPE: gloves, nose masks, goggles, boots

Mechanization Services

  • Tractor hiring
  • Ploughing, harrowing, planting
  • Spraying and harvesting
  • Trained operators, charged per hectare

Quality Assurance

All inputs are sourced from certified manufacturers and duly registered. We also train farmers on safe and effective use.
